# Store listing

Copy for both stores, and the assets each one needs. Written to be true: every
claim here is one the app can be held to, which is the only kind worth making
about privacy.

## Name and subtitle

- **Name**: TXNS
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30 chars)**: Spending, kept on your phone
- **Short description (Play, 80 chars)**: A spending tracker that keeps your money data on your device, not a server.

## Description

> Track what you spend without handing your finances to anyone.
>
> TXNS keeps your ledger on your phone. Not on our servers — we do not have
> any. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no sync service reading
> your transactions, because there is nothing on the other end to read them.
>
> **Capture the way that suits the moment.** Photograph a receipt and the total,
> date and merchant are read on the device. Say what you spent and it is
> transcribed on the device, by a speech model that runs with no network at all.
> Or just type it — the keypad opens on the amount.
>
> **Bring in your statements.** Import a CSV or a PDF from your bank in the web
> app. Rows you have already imported are recognised, so importing the same file
> twice does not duplicate a thing.
>
> **Backups you control.** Your backup is encrypted on the device with a
> password only you know, and a recovery key shown once. Keep it on the phone or
> put it in your own Google Drive; either way it is unreadable without your
> password — to us, to Google, to anyone who takes the file.
>
> **What leaves your phone:** nothing, unless you ask. Optional Drive backup
> goes to your own account, already encrypted. Optional Cloud Assist sends
> redacted text — never your photos, your recordings or your ledger — to an AI
> provider you choose, with your own API key, and only after you have read the
> exact text and tapped send. Both are off until you turn them on.
>
> **Works with no signal.** Every screen, every capture and every total works in
> airplane mode, because none of it was ever waiting on a network.

## Screenshots

2. The keypad on the add screen, mid-amount.
3. A receipt photo with the total read from it and its evidence line showing.
4. The voice screen, transcript visible, "Use this" ready.
5. The backup screen at the recovery-key step, with the "no reset" warning.
6. The summary screen by category.

Capture them from a device with the seeded ledger, not from a simulator with
one row. Every amount visible must be invented.

## What must not appear in the listing

- Any claim of end-to-end *sync*. There is no sync; there are backups.
- Any promise about Cloud Assist's provider — that is the user's choice and
  their contract, not ours.
- Screenshots containing a real person's spending.
